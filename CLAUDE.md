# AI Research Bot Protocol

## Goals
Scan for high-signal updates in AI Safety, Governance, and Interpretability.

## Sources & Entities
- **Primary Sources:** arXiv (cs.AI, cs.LG), alignmentforum.org
- **Key Organizations:** Anthropic, DeepMind Safety, GovAI, METR
- **Key Researchers:** Jan Leike, Amanda Askell, Chris Olah, Anca Dragan, Pushmeet Kohli, Beth Barnes

## Daily Tasks
1. **Write** a research briefing based on your training knowledge about recent developments in AI safety, interpretability, and governance.
2. **Filter:** Prioritize topics related to the "Key Researchers" or "Key Organizations" listed above.
3. **Save** the output to `reports/daily-briefing-YYYY-MM-DD.md` using today's actual date.
4. **Format:** For each relevant topic, provide:
   - **Title & Topic Area**
   - **The "So What?":** 2 sentences on why this matters for safety/governance.
   - **Technical Core:** A brief summary of the key ideas.
   - **Relationship:** Mention if it relates to any of the key researchers or organizations.

## Constraints
- If no high-signal topics are found, write "No critical updates today." into the file.
- Do not summarize general "hype" or marketing-heavy AI news.
- Always write content to the file — never leave it empty.
- You do not have internet access. Use your training knowledge only.
