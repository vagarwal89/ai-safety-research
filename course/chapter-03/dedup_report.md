## What this means

Every removed story is text the model would otherwise see twice. Duplicates do three kinds of harm: they waste training compute, they push the model toward memorizing the repeated text (Chapter 6 measures that directly), and if a duplicated story lands in both training and test data, your evaluation is lying to you. A dedup pass is cheap insurance against all three.

The threshold is a judgment call, not a law of nature. Lower it and you remove more, including stories that are merely similar. Raise it and near-copies slip through. Record the threshold you used: it is part of your Article 10(2)(c) preparation record.
