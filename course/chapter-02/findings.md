## Exercise B: Purpose-limitation test

### Customer-service chatbot (support chat logs)

1. Original purpose (one sentence): The chat logs were collected to resolve that customer's support request.
2. Training purpose (one sentence): The model will learn from the logs to answer future customers' questions.
3. Article 6(4) factors:
   - Link between the purposes: Related but not the same. Both are customer service, but one helps this customer now and the other builds a product for all future customers.
   - Context of collection: Customers gave their details to get help, not to train a model, so they would not reasonably expect their chat to become training data.
   - Nature of the data: The logs include names, email addresses, order history, and home addresses, which are direct identifiers, and sometimes payment details.
   - Consequences for the person: If the model memorizes a log and repeats an address or order detail to another customer, that is a real leak, and the customer loses control of data they shared in confidence.
   - Safeguards you would apply: Pseudonymize the logs first, meaning replace names, emails, and addresses with tokens, minimize by dropping order numbers and payment details and keeping only the question and answer pattern, and delete the raw logs after training
4. Verdict (compatible / compatible with safeguards / not compatible): compatible with safeguards. Not a clean compatible, because the purposes differ and customers never expected training use. Not incompatible either, because the link is genuine and the safeguards close the gap. This middle verdict is the most common real answer in governance work.
