## Comparison

TinyStories declares CDLA Sharing 1.0 in its Hugging Face metadata, but the card text itself never discusses licensing, and an independent audit (Longpre et al., Nature Machine Intelligence, 2024) found a conflicting CC BY-NC 4.0 label for the same dataset. Two sources, two answers. SQuAD declares CC BY-SA 4.0 in one line on its dataset card. That contrast is the governance finding: the same audit method, two very different answers. When sources conflict, record both and use the stricter one until you can resolve it.
